Masroofi

A bilingual personal-finance app for the web and Android.

Stack
Laravel 13, PHP 8.5, Hotwire Turbo, Hotwire Native, Laravel MCP, Pest
Links
Live app

What it does

  • Books in any currency: accounts, transfers, refunds, opening balances and nested categories, kept as double entry.
  • Statement import: upload a bank PDF or forward it by email. Three banks are read directly; others come in through an AI assistant and are accepted only if the running balances add up.
  • Budgets and recurring entries. A new budget can start from last month or last year’s spending.
  • Gold and silver held in grams, valued at market rates, with gains before and after charges.
  • Zakat due on the Hijri anniversary, with a gold or silver nisab.
  • Lend and borrow, including split bills.
  • Arabic and English, right to left included, in light and dark.

How it’s built

  • Laravel 13 on PHP 8.5 with Hotwire Turbo: server-rendered pages that feel like an app.
  • The Android app wraps the same pages with Hotwire Native and adds native tabs, push notifications and a fingerprint lock.
  • Email import runs through a signed Mailgun webhook and a queued job; the user gets an email and a push when it’s done.
  • Matching runs in steps: the same amount within three days, then transfers between your own accounts, then your rules. Anything left waits for review.
  • An MCP server behind OAuth gives AI assistants 19 tools to read balances, record transactions and import statements. Editing and deleting are left out on purpose.
  • Money is stored as whole numbers in the smallest unit.

Quality

  • 150 test files with about 660 cases, including browser tests.
  • PHPStan at the highest level, 100% type coverage, and Rector and Pint on every commit.

Result

Live at masroofi.yehya.dev and on Google Play.
